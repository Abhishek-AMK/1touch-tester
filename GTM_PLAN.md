# Go-to-Market Plan — Apex-Mixed-Upgrade

## Product
**Apex-Mixed-Upgrade** — the next advanced version of the Apex Growth Labs personal task board.

## Market Context (as of 2026-09-18)
The lightweight personal task-board market is dominated by freemium SaaS tools that are powerful but increasingly paywalled and account-centric:

- **Todoist** — task-list leader with board view. Free "Beginner" plan is limited to 5 projects, 3 filters, and 1-week activity history. Pro is $5/month billed annually ($60/year) or $7/month monthly; Business is $8/user/month annually. Sources checked August 6, 2026.
  - Inspectable sources: https://saascrmreview.com/todoist-review and https://www.usecarly.com/blog/todoist-pricing
- **Trello** — kanban pioneer. Free plan includes unlimited cards but caps users to 10 boards per workspace, 10 collaborators, and 250 automation runs/month. Standard is $5/user/month annually; Premium is $10/user/month annually; Enterprise starts at $17.50/user/month annually. Source verified April 24, 2026 and re-verified September 2026.
  - Inspectable sources: https://factgrid.org/entities/trello and https://www.empyra.com/trello-pricing

Opportunity: solo users, indie hackers, and consultants want a **fast, private, no-account board** that persists locally without subscriptions or collaboration friction.

## Positioning
**"The distraction-free personal task board that lives on your machine."**

- No signup, no cloud, no subscription.
- Open/done filters to focus on what matters now.
- Persistent local storage across restarts.
- Clean, lightweight UI with a live UTC clock.

## Target User
- Solo consultants and freelancers managing client deliverables.
- Indie hackers and developers who want a local-first task board.
- Anyone burned by freemium limits (project caps, filter limits, automation caps).

## Three Campaign Ideas
1. **"Escape the 5-Project Cap"** — Compare Apex-Mixed-Upgrade against Todoist’s free project limit. Emphasize unlimited local boards/tasks with no account.
2. **"Your Tasks, Your Machine"** — Privacy-first messaging. Target users frustrated by cloud sync, AI data training, and vendor lock-in.
3. **"Open Source Task Board in 30 Seconds"** — GitHub + npm install campaign for developers. Position it as a hackable, self-hosted alternative to Trello’s free-tier board limits.
